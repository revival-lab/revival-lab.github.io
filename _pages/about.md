Code Revival Lab designs and builds new technical software and helps teams
modernise existing systems. Our work includes embedded firmware, microservices,
scientific software, and performance-sensitive applications.

We use AI tools when a client chooses to use them, with critical
human review and verification throughout the work. AI use is optional; we can
also deliver projects without it. We agree on the approach with each client.

Mikael Lund, founder of Code Revival Lab in 2023, is a seasoned software developer with a diverse background.
Growing up with assembler in the late 1980s, he was active in the demo scene on Commodore 64 and the Amiga.
Over the past two decades, he has specialised in scientific software development and high-performance computing.
Additionally, Mikael has contributed to Rust-based projects, particularly in embedded systems.
